## The idea

Good ideas rarely arrive when we're sitting down to work.

They happen in the shower, during conversations, on walks,
and at 2 AM when we should probably be sleeping.

But between scattered notes, screenshots, voice memos
and forgotten documents, our best ideas often get lost.

I built Top Shelf to change that.

## What is Top Shelf?

Top Shelf is an AI-powered idea management platform
designed to help curious, creative people capture,
connect, validate and act on their ideas.

It's not another notes app. It's a home for your thinking.

## Key features

- **Idea capture** — Give every idea a place to live.
- **Aisles** — Organize your thoughts without traditional folders.
- **AI-powered connections** — Discover relationships between ideas.
- **Research and validation** — Explore ideas with supporting sources.
- **Action plans** — Turn ideas into actionable next steps.
- **Idea validation** — Gather feedback to make informed decisions.
